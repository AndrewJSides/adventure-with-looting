# Adventure with Looting — Roadmap

Set by Andrew, Oct 5, 2026. Vision: like **Surroundead** (Steam indie) — open world with zombies, looting buildings, bosses scattered about, traveling around finding rare loot.

## Roadmap (in rough priority order)
1. **Vehicles** — drivable vehicles for crossing the huge map (first up).
2. **Pet dog** — a companion dog that follows/fights with the player.
3. **Machine guns** — automatic ranged weapons alongside the current tap-to-shoot.
4. **Swords and other melee weapons** — expand melee beyond the single slash attack; lootable/craftable melee weapons.
5. **Bases** — player-buildable/claimable bases (ties into the existing house + trunk).
6. **Dungeons** — instanced or delimited dungeon areas with elites/bosses and rare loot.
7. **Key-locked chests** — chests that require finding rare keys to open.
8. **Expansive cities** — both inhabited (NPCs, shops, quests) and abandoned (lootable buildings, zombie hordes, bosses).
9. **Day/night cycle** — full cycle affecting visibility and zombie behavior.
10. **Flashlights** — findable/usable flashlights (pairs with day/night).
11. **Zombie sound effects** — groans, screams, ambient horror audio.
12. **Way more NPCs and dialogue** — after cities land.
13. **Deeper storyline** — after 12 (which follows 8).

## Already shipped (context)
- 11+ biomes, ~16× world scale, zombie enemies with biome variants, elites, named bosses with unique relic drops
- Inventory grid with icons, trinket slot, player house + storage trunk (village spawn)
- NPCs: Hearthglen villagers, Sable (quest line), Maro's shop; campfire/wayfire fast travel
- Minimap with fog of war, terrain blending, region labels; mobile-first touch controls
